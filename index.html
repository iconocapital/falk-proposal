How we help

Nine specific jobs we take on.

Each one ties to something you said in the May 19 meeting. Nothing generic, nothing theoretical. Items marked ESSENTIALS are only included in the ongoing engagement.

01

Cash Deployment

Move the $185K savings into a tiered structure: $100K reserve (your number), $40K for quarterly tax bills, the rest into the portfolio.

Stop earning bank rates on $85K
02

Inherited IRA Audit

The $407K Bene IRA at UMB / Columbia Threadneedle is holding mutual funds at expense ratios above 1%. That's more than what we'd charge to manage them.

Annual fee drag, quantified
03

RMD Recalculation

Your mom passed in 2000 before 72½, so the RMD is on your life expectancy. We confirm the exact figure each year. The IRS penalty for getting it wrong is 50% of the shortfall.

Removes the audit risk
04

Asset Location Repair

Bonds and cash sitting in the Roth IRA are the wrong fit. Roth is where the most aggressive growth assets belong. Swap them with equity from the SEP.

Tax-free space, used right
05

Portfolio Reallocation

Shift from 72/28 to 60/40. Cut the 5% Nvidia / 5% Microsoft concentration to 2-3% each. Add international (0% → 13%) and emerging markets (0.3% → 2.4%).

Risk you need, not risk you bear
06

SEP vs. Roth Withdrawal Sequencing

The "I don't understand when to take from what" problem. Build the ordering logic: which account funds which year, how Social Security fits, where the inherited RMD slots in.

The map you've asked for
07

Self-Employment Tax Review

Pull the last two years of returns and look for levers — SEP vs. Solo 401(k) contribution capacity, S-corp election math, QBI / 199A treatment, home office and equipment. You're paying around $66K in federal & state today; some of that is recoverable.

Stops April from stinging
08

Long-Term Care Strategy

Your stated fear, ending up in a home, is the planning question we take most seriously. Evaluate hybrid LTC policies now (they're still cost-effective at 59) or self-insure with an explicit reserve.

Names the biggest unknown
09

Estate Basics

Single, no kids, but you have $2.2M. We confirm the will, the healthcare proxy, the beneficiary designations across four accounts, and the apartment titling are all aligned.

Protects the autonomy, in writing
10

Ongoing Execution

Quarterly rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting in the SEP and Bene IRA, annual plan reviews, and Jack monitoring concentration drift across all four accounts.

The plan, continuously run
11

Proactive Industry Outreach

When the next job runs short, you don't email us asking what to do. We're already on it. Cash flow stress-testing happens as the work changes, not after.
